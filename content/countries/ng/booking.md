# Booking your Chief Mate CoC exam — Nigeria

> **DRAFT. Every step, document and fee below must be confirmed with NIMASA (Nigerian Maritime Administration and Safety Agency) before you rely on it.** This page shows the *typical* shape of the process so you can prepare early.

## 1. Check you are eligible
- Hold a valid OOW (STCW II/1) certificate of competency.
- Have the qualifying sea service in an OOW capacity. **The required months for Nigeria are not confirmed yet.** Track yours under *More → Tracker*.
- Hold the management-level STCW training certificates your administration requires. Commonly these include medical care, advanced fire fighting, ECDIS and leadership/managerial courses. **The list is not confirmed for Nigeria.**

## 2. Gather documents (typical)
- Passport and seafarer's ID / discharge book
- Sea service testimonials signed by the Master or company
- Current CoC and endorsements
- Course certificates and medical fitness certificate
- Passport photographs

## 3. Apply to NIMASA
- Submit the application and pay the fee (**amount not confirmed**).
- Book written and oral exam dates (format **not confirmed**).

## 4. Prepare
- Clear the app's **Exam-ready** bars: 3 mocks at 85% or more and 3 orals at 80% or more with no critical failure.
- Practise orals out loud in voice mode.

## 5. After the exam
- Keep copies of results. Apply for issue of the CoC and any endorsements.
